    About Four

    Four in a row, for two people wherever they are. There are no accounts, no ads and no saved games: just a board and a friend.

    Starting a game

    1. Choose who goes first, then Start a game.
    2. Send the code (or the link) to your friend.
    3. When they join, the game begins.

    No one to play? Pick Easy, Medium or Hard and choose Play the computer. It plays right here in your browser.

    Playing

    • Take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest empty space.
    • Get four of your discs in a row, across, up and down, or diagonally, to win.
    • If the board fills up with no four in a row, it’s a draw.
    • Tap or click a column to drop. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys and Enter, or press 1 to 7.
    • Play again starts a rematch. Whoever lost goes first; after a draw, you swap.

    Video and chat

    Turn on your camera or microphone at any time during a game, or just type. Each of you decides separately what to share.

    Privacy

    Four is a static page. A public PeerJS server introduces your two browsers using the game code; after that, moves, chat, video and audio travel directly between you over WebRTC. Nothing is recorded, and only your color settings are saved, in this browser.

    Resign this game?

    Your opponent wins. You can play again afterwards.

    Leave this game?

    The game ends for both of you and can’t be resumed.

    Connection lost

    The link to your opponent dropped.
